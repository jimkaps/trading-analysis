# Findings, 24 Nov 2026

Work done on 24 Nov 2026: a check of every TradingView script in use, fixes, and the tests that decided the
current strategy line-up ([STRATEGIES.md](STRATEGIES.md)). Data: Dukascopy 1-minute Nasdaq-100 (index, not
futures) and TradingView's own backtests. Costs on MNQ: 2 ticks slippage per side + $1.24 per round trip.

## 1. TradingView script check

Seven scripts were on the "Unnamed" MNQ1! 5-minute layout. All compiled; most had problems:

- **BB Breakout + TP/SL/Trail:** trail passed in price instead of ticks (5-pt trail on MNQ, $5 on BTC);
  plotted trail and alerts didn't match the real order; 150-tick slippage (37.5 pts per side on MNQ).
- **BTC 4H Donchian v7, H4:** fractional position size rounds to 0 contracts on futures, so they never traded;
  H4 also had 100-tick slippage, an unguarded loop and legs that closed each other.
- **NQ ORB combo (prop):** unguarded open-trades loop; $125 risk sized most setups to 0 contracts.
- **BB Breakout No Trail v1:** no commission or slippage.
- Several chart copies were older drafts than the saved scripts.

Fixed copies are in `tv_fixed/` (README there has before/after backtests). `nq_orb_strategy.pine` slippage was
raised from 1 to 2 ticks. Python and TradingView agree on preset E trade by trade over Aug–Nov 2026 (99% of
days win/lose the same way, correlation 0.99).
